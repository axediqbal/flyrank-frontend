# FL-04 — Five runs of the Outreach Opener Pipeline

Inputs are real leads from Ahmed's outreach lists (Business List 3 + the
sniper-list research, Oct 2026). Nothing in any fact sheet was invented;
fields the lists did not confirm are marked UNCONFIRMED and never used.
Each run followed the project instructions exactly: Gather → Angle → Draft
→ Critique → (human gate) → Revise.

---

## Run 1 — Boss Cafe (cafe)

**Fact sheet.** Cafe. Running Facebook/Instagram ads; no working website —
ad traffic has nowhere to land. A one-page demo site has already been built
for this business. UNCONFIRMED: prices, hours, exact address.

**Angle.** They already pay for attention; the ads point at no website, so
the click ends at a social profile instead of a menu and a booking path.
Proof: the finished demo.

**Draft.** "Salam, I saw Boss Cafe's ads running — but they land on your
Instagram, not a website, so that ad spend is mostly wasted. I build
websites for cafes; I actually made a one-page demo for Boss Cafe already,
with your menu and booking front and centre. Can I send you the link? It
takes two minutes to look at."

**Critique.** REVISE. (b)/(e) "that ad spend is mostly wasted" — a judgment
about their results, not a fact in the sheet; the opener's job is the
observation, not the verdict. Everything else passes: one CTA, facts only.

**Final opener.** "Salam, I saw Boss Cafe's ads running — they land on your
Instagram, not a website of your own. I build websites for cafes, and I've
already made a one-page demo for Boss Cafe: menu and booking details up
front, in your branding. Can I send you the link? Two minutes to look at,
and it's yours to judge."

## Run 2 — Activo Fitness (gym, Lahore)

**Fact sheet.** Gym, DHA Phase 4 (199-FF Commercial), Lahore. 4.3 rating,
331 reviews. Range from yoga to kickboxing. No website found; joining means
visiting or calling. UNCONFIRMED: prices, timings, trainer names.

**Angle.** 331 reviews' worth of proven demand, and no page a new member
can join or check classes from. Offer: one-page demo.

**Draft.** "Salam, Activo has 331 reviews and a 4.3 rating in DHA Phase 4 —
but no website, so someone who finds you still has to call or visit just to
see classes and join. I build websites for gyms. I can make Activo a
one-page demo with your class range and a join button. Can I send it over,
or would a 5-minute call suit you better?"

**Critique.** REVISE. (c) two calls to action — "send it over" OR "a call" —
the reader is asked to choose instead of act. Pick the demo send; the call
is a later step.

**Final opener.** "Salam, Activo has 331 reviews and a 4.3 rating in DHA
Phase 4 — but no website, so someone who finds you still has to call or
visit just to see classes and join. I build websites for gyms, and I can
make Activo a one-page demo with your class range and a clear join button.
Can I send it over?"

## Run 3 — Black Rock Cafe (cafe, Islamabad)

**Fact sheet.** Cafe, E-11/2, Islamabad. 4.2 rating, ~1,500 reviews. The
"website" field online points to Instagram only; reservations happen by
phone call. UNCONFIRMED: prices, hours, menu details.

**Angle.** A 1,500-review cafe whose only booking path is a phone call and
whose menu lives in Instagram posts. Proof: the live Lumière restaurant
site (menu + reservations).

**Draft.** "Salam, Black Rock has about 1,500 reviews — but reservations
still run through phone calls, and there's no website of your own; the link
online just opens Instagram. I build websites for cafes and restaurants.
Here's one I shipped that's live now, with its menu and reservations on it:
lamier.vercel.app. If that shape suits Black Rock, I can build yours the
same way. Can I send you a one-page demo?"

**Critique.** PASS. Facts trace to the sheet; the proof is real and
relevant; one CTA; no superlatives; 69 words.

**Final opener.** As drafted.

## Run 4 — GET FIT (gym, Lahore)

**Fact sheet.** Boutique gym, DHA Phase 3 (103-Y), Lahore. 4.8 rating.
Online presence is a directory listing only — no website, no membership
page. UNCONFIRMED: prices, class schedule, facilities list.

**Angle.** The strongest rating on this list, discoverable only through
directories; a "gym DHA Phase 3" searcher finds listings, not a page that
can convert them. Offer: one-page demo.

**Draft.** "Salam, GET FIT is the best-rated gym in DHA — 4.8 — yet online
you're only a directory listing, so searchers can't see memberships or join
from anywhere. I build websites for gyms and can make you a one-page demo
with memberships and a join button. Can I send it over?"

**Critique.** REVISE. (a) "the best-rated gym in DHA" — a superlative built
on one directory's rating; the sheet supports "4.8 rating", not a ranking.
Quote the fact, drop the crown.

**Final opener.** "Salam, GET FIT holds a 4.8 rating in DHA Phase 3 — yet
online you're only a directory listing, so someone searching can't see
memberships or join from a page of your own. I build websites for gyms,
and I can make GET FIT a one-page demo with memberships and a join button.
Can I send it over?"

## Run 5 — Artisan Aesthetics, Dr. Ainy Sohail (clinic, Lahore)

**Fact sheet.** Aesthetics clinic, Johar Town (opposite Emporium Mall),
Lahore. Treatment videos on YouTube; no website. Bookings happen over
WhatsApp. UNCONFIRMED: treatment prices, doctor's schedule, full treatment
list.

**Angle.** Video content already creates the demand; the booking path is a
WhatsApp conversation with a wait built in. Offer: one-page demo with
treatments and a booking button.

**Draft.** "Salam, your treatment videos on YouTube do the hard work of
convincing patients — but booking still means waiting on WhatsApp, which
patients must find frustrating. I build websites for clinics; I can make
Artisan Aesthetics a one-page demo listing your treatments with a booking
button. Can I send it over?"

**Critique.** REVISE. (e) "which patients must find frustrating" — assumes
feelings no fact states. The sheet supports the wait (WhatsApp-only
booking), not the frustration. State the path, not the emotion.

**Final opener.** "Salam, your treatment videos on YouTube do the hard work
of convincing patients — but booking still runs entirely through WhatsApp.
I build websites for clinics, and I can make Artisan Aesthetics a one-page
demo: your treatments listed, with a booking button up front. Can I send it
over?"

## What the five runs showed

- The critique step earned its place: 3 of 5 drafts failed on first pass —
  a judgment dressed as a fact (Run 1), two CTAs (Run 2), a superlative
  (Run 4), an assumed emotion (Run 5). All four failure types are on the
  Step 4 checklist because FL-02's ladder produced them first.
- Two gyms (Runs 2 and 4) produced near-identical angles — "reviews but no
  page." True for both, but a human varies the second one's proof or CTA so
  the batch doesn't read as one template. The pipeline flags facts; taste
  is still a human step.
- Every run stayed inside its fact sheet: no price, hour, or service was
  invented, because Step 1 restates only what was given and Step 4 quotes
  any line that leaves the sheet.
