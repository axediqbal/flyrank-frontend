   performance.
2. **What you did** — the shader itself (GLSL, `u_time` /
   `u_resolution` / `u_mouse`), the readability guard (text contrast

3. **What came of it** — the live URL, the Lighthouse numbers after
   shipping it, and the reviewer outcome once submitted.
